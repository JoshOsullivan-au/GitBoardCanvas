# Phase 01: first phase

## Overview

What the phase is, in two sentences.

## Directive

What the builder must do.

## Objective/Goal

The outcome when the phase lands.

## Limitations/Restrictions

What is out of reach or out of scope.

## Guardrails

Rules that hold throughout the phase.

## Done when

Checkable conditions. Each one can be answered yes or no.

## Orchestration

Who or what runs each step, and in what order.

## Depends on

Phases and facts that must exist first. Write `none` if nothing does.

## Steps

Ordered actions. Each one ends on a completion criterion.

## UI

Screens and components, with a link to the approved mock. Write `none` when there is no UI.

## Files/references

Files to change and sources to read.

## Review

Who checks the work. The builder does not close their own phase.

## Loop

A failed Verify or Review returns to Orchestration. After three failures on the same criterion, hand it to a person.

## Execution

A log of what was done, with file and commit references.

## Verify

The commands and checks that prove Done when, with their output.
